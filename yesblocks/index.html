Get the game

How it plays

One number. One rectangle.

Match the clue

Each number belongs to exactly one rectangle, and the rectangle covers that many cells. Nothing more.

Mind the holes

Some squares are missing on purpose. A finished board fills every open cell and leaves those gaps empty.

Stuck? Spend a coin

Clearing a level earns coins. A hint spends them and shows one correct shape. Your progress stays on the device.

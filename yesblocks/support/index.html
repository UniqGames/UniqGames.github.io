Support

Help for Yes! Blocks, and a direct line to the team.

Need a person? Email uniqgames@gmail.com with "Yes! Blocks" in the subject. We read every message and reply within two working days. Include the details under reporting a problem.

Playing the game

How do I play?

Each number on the board is a clue. Drag a rectangle that covers exactly that many empty cells and contains only that one number. Fill every open cell. Leave the holes empty.

If a rectangle is wrong, it breaks back into cells and you can try again. A hint spends coins and shows one correct shape.

What is the difference between normal, hard, and super hard?

Later levels mix more rectangle sizes and larger boards. Harder levels award more coins when you clear them: 5 on a normal level, 7 on a hard level, and 9 on a super hard level.

Progress and your data

Where is my progress saved?

On the device, inside the app. Yes! Blocks does not have an account, a sign-in, or a cloud save. Level, score, coins, the tutorial flag, and your music, sound, and haptic settings stay in local storage.

If you delete the app or clear its data, that save is gone and we cannot restore it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete Yes! Blocks from your device, or clear the app's storage in system settings. That removes the local save.

The game also sends anonymous product-analytics events to Firebase. To ask about those records, email uniqgames@gmail.com. There is no player account to delete. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I request a copy of my data?

Email uniqgames@gmail.com. Most of the save never leaves the phone. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law that applies to you requires it.

Technical problems

The game crashes, freezes, or will not load.

Close and reopen the app, restart the device, and install the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. Then send us a report with the details below.

Sound or haptics are not working.

Open the gear on the home screen or during a level. Music, sound, and haptics each have their own switch. Also check that the device is not silenced and that system haptics are allowed.

Reporting a problem

Email uniqgames@gmail.com. These details make the first reply useful:

  • That the message is about Yes! Blocks
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • The game version from the store page
  • The level number, if the problem is on one level
  • What you expected, and what happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can capture one

Privacy preferences

Your data choices in one place — what we collect, what you can switch off, and where each control lives.

Choices on this website

These settings apply to this browser on this device. They are stored locally and never sent to us. The website itself sets no cookies; the full list is in the Cookie Policy.

Strictly necessary

Needed to serve the pages you asked for and to remember the choices on this screen. Cannot be switched off.

Website analytics

We do not currently run any analytics on this website. If we ever add it, this switch decides whether it applies to you, and it will default to off.

Marketing communications

Whether we may contact you about launches and updates, if you have given us an email address. Off unless you turn it on.

Choices in the game

Monster Trip contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking, so there is no advertising profile to opt out of. The SDKs it uses, and how long they keep identifiers, are listed in the Cookie Policy. The data those SDKs collect falls into three groups.

CategoryWhat it coversWhere you control it
Essential Your account, saved progress, gold and lives, leaderboard entry and team membership. Cannot be switched off while you have an account — the game cannot function without it. Deleting your account removes it all.
Analytics and performance Anonymous, aggregated events such as levels started and completed and boosters used, which tell us where the difficulty curve is wrong. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Android: Settings → Google → Ads, and reset or opt out of the advertising ID.
Crash diagnostics Crash reports and error logs, used only to find and fix faults. Covered by the same device-level analytics settings above.

Notifications

Open Settings in the game to switch notifications on or off, and check your device's system settings for the app as well. Both have to be enabled for a notification to reach you.

Parental controls

Under Settings → Parental Control you can disable purchases and disable chat and social features, then lock both behind a PIN.

Deleting your data

The most complete control available to you is deletion. In the game, open Settings → Terms & Privacy → Delete Account. This removes your account, progress, profile, leaderboard entries and team membership.

Deletion is carried out 30 days after you ask for it. Opening the game again within those 30 days cancels the request and restores your account exactly as you left it. After that the removal is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you cannot reach that option, email uniqgames@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will do it for you.

More detail

The full picture is in our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. If anything is unclear, ask us at uniqgames@gmail.com — we would rather explain it than have you guess.

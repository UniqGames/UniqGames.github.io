About us

A small studio with a long horizon

Uniq Games was founded on a simple belief: a casual puzzle game deserves the same craft as a console title. We keep the team small, own every discipline in-house, and ship only what we would happily play on our own commute.

Craft over volume

One game at a time, tuned until the moment-to-moment feel is right. Every animation curve, sound and reward beat is deliberate.

Characters first

Our worlds start with the cast. If a character is not fun to watch idle on the home screen, the feature around them is not finished.

Fair by default

No dark patterns, no pressure. Parental controls, clear pricing and honest difficulty are part of the design, not an afterthought.
